# Editorial and technical standards

## Default master

- 1920x1080, 16:9, 30 fps
- H.264 video, AAC stereo audio, MP4 container
- 48 kHz audio
- Planned duration 4:40–4:50; absolute project maximum configurable up to 300 seconds
- Thai text inside title-safe margins with high contrast

## Picture

- Preserve aspect ratio and pad when needed.
- Correct exposure conservatively; avoid crushed blacks, clipped highlights, or unnatural saturation.
- Use close-ups of evidence only when text remains readable at 1080p.
- Limit transitions to short fades or cuts unless motion conveys information.

## Sound

- Prioritize narration over music and location sound.
- Use automatic ducking when voice-over and music overlap.
- Avoid clipping; normalize the final mix conservatively.
- Fade music at the beginning and end.

## Text and identity

- Verify the school name, district, director, presenters, dates, and Thai spelling against source documents.
- Use the original school logo without redrawing or changing proportions.
- Keep lower thirds long enough to read and avoid covering evidence.

## Child safety and rights

- Confirm permission and intended audience before showing identifiable children.
- Minimize student personal data and sensitive incident information.
- Blur or replace material that exposes IDs, addresses, health information, discipline records, or private contact details.
- Use licensed, public-domain, or school-owned music and imagery. Record the source or license in the production notes.
