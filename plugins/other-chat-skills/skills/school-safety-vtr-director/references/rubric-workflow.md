# Rubric-to-evidence workflow

## Source priority

1. Current district or committee rubric supplied by the user
2. Current official national guidance explicitly named by that rubric
3. School records and dated evidence
4. General best practice, clearly labeled as advice rather than a scored requirement

## Required matrix

Create one row per scored criterion:

| ID | Criterion | Weight | Required evidence | Available media | Timecode | Narration claim | Status |
|---|---|---:|---|---|---|---|---|

Use `Ready`, `Partial`, `Missing`, or `Not applicable` for status. Never mark `Ready` unless the visible or audible evidence supports the narration claim.

## Story allocation

- Allocate time by score weight and evidence strength.
- Show evidence while the corresponding claim is spoken.
- Prefer observable action, dated records, drills, inspections, student participation, community partnership, and measured outcomes over decorative shots.
- Keep opening identity material brief unless it is explicitly scored.
- End with verified outcomes and continuity, not unsupported superlatives.

## Deliverables before editing

- Evidence matrix
- Missing-evidence list
- Thai narration script with estimated duration
- Scene storyboard with time ranges
- Shot list for missing or weak visuals
- Approved media inventory
