# Committee-style quality check

## Gate checks

- Output opens and contains video and audio streams.
- Duration does not exceed `max_duration_seconds`.
- No black gaps, frozen unintended frames, missing audio, or truncated ending.
- School and personnel names match authoritative sources.
- Every narration claim is supported by visible evidence or an identified record.
- No required criterion is omitted without being flagged.
- Student privacy and media rights have been reviewed.

## Scored review

Rate each area 0–2 and explain every score below 2:

1. Rubric coverage
2. Evidence visibility
3. Factual accuracy
4. Story clarity
5. Narration intelligibility
6. Picture quality
7. Text legibility
8. Technical compliance
9. Privacy and copyright compliance
10. Credible outcomes and continuity

Require revision for any gate failure, any unsupported claim, or any total below 17/20. Report fixes with exact timecodes.
