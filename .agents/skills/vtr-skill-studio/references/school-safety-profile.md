# School-safety VTR profile

## Purpose

Present verified school-safety performance to a district assessment committee in no more than five minutes.

## Authority

Use the current district rubric supplied by the school as the primary source. Use official national guidance only when referenced by the rubric or clearly labeled as supporting guidance.

## Production defaults

- Hard limit: 300 seconds
- Target: 280–290 seconds
- Evidence-to-criterion matrix required before scripting
- Show evidence at the same time as the related narration claim
- Allocate time by score weight and evidence strength
- Keep school introduction brief unless scored
- Finish with verified outcomes and continuity

## Required controls

- No unsupported claim receives a `Ready` status.
- Flag missing or weak evidence before rendering.
- Verify names, dates, activities, drills, inspections, partnerships, and outcomes.
- Protect identifiable students and sensitive incident information.
- Conduct both technical QC and a simulated committee review.

Use `school-safety-vtr-director` for production and rendering.
