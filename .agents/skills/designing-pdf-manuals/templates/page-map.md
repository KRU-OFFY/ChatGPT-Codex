## Page-Level Rules

For every page define:
- one dominant communication goal,
- required facts and source,
- exact image/diagram purpose,
- layout archetype,
- blocking QA criterion.

Do not mark a page `READY_FOR_LAYOUT` until its required source and visual plan are known.
