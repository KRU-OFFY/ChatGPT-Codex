# Learning Sidecar

Resolve and read TOFFY Skill Engineering Standard by exact frontmatter name, including its learning-record reference. Follow its promotion gates. Store project incidents in the authorized durable project record; do not write secrets or student identities into reusable skill files.

Record incident ID/time, command/source/artifact versions, authorized scope, tool readiness, input/action, expected/actual, raw evidence and FAILED/SUCCEEDED/PARTIAL/UNKNOWN. Separate observed facts, inferred causes, proposed remedies and verified outcomes.

For each candidate improvement, record linked incidents, root-cause tests, baseline/candidate diff, RED/GREEN, diverse cases, regression, independent evaluation, acceptance decision, rollback and later recurrence. Do not promote when a required gate is missing. User-authorized operating rules are requirements, not empirical proof that errors decreased.

Check related verified lessons before later work and record actual recurrence or success. Do not claim background monitoring or access to every historical conversation. Continue collection only during accessible authorized work.
